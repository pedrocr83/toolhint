# Vendor quotes for routing software, six sites

Collected by Procurement, 12 May 2026. Both quotes are for a 3-year minimum term, prices excluding VAT.

## RouteLoom (the vendor we piloted)
- **Subscription:** €214,000 per year for all six sites.
- **One-time integration:** €38,000, covering connector installation and WMS mapping at the five new sites.
- **Compatibility:** works with our current WMS v8, as proven in the pilot.
- **Rollout plan:** two waves of about 8 weeks each. If the contract is signed by 15 July 2026, all six sites are live by mid-October 2026.
- **SLA:** 99.5% monthly availability, with service credits of 5% of the monthly fee for each 0.5% below target.

## Pathwise
- **Subscription:** €176,000 per year for all six sites.
- **One-time integration:** €95,000.
- **Compatibility:** requires WMS v9. Pathwise does not support v8.
- **Evidence:** no pilot with us. Pathwise cites a 15 to 20% pick-rate gain at two reference customers, both single-site.
- **Offer:** a free 3-month trial at one site, starting once we are on WMS v9.
- **SLA:** 99.9% monthly availability.

## Procurement note
Over three years, subscription plus integration comes to €680,000 for RouteLoom and €623,000 for Pathwise.
