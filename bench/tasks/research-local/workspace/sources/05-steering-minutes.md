# Warehouse routing steering group, minutes of 20 May 2026

**Present:** Ines Vermeer (COO, chair), Tomas Kral (CFO), Hanna Lindqvist (Operations Director), Sipho Dlamini (IT Lead), Marc Okafor (Sales Director)

## Decision criteria agreed
1. **Payback:** the CFO requires the chosen option to pay back within 18 months, counting one-time costs.
2. **Timing:** the Operations Director needs every site live before peak season, which starts on 2 November 2026. A rollout during peak is not acceptable.
3. **Evidence:** a gain measured in our own warehouses carries more weight than vendor references.

## Discussion
- **IT:** Pathwise needs WMS v9. The v9 upgrade is budgeted, but the WMS vendor has scheduled it for Q1 2027 and cannot bring it forward because of their capacity. The upgrade cost is in the IT budget either way, so it does not count against Pathwise.
- **HR (by email):** routing software records per-picker performance. At the Rotterdam and Liège sites that requires consultation with the works council first, which takes 6 to 8 weeks.
- **Legal:** RouteLoom's contract has a 3-year minimum term. Legal will ask whether an exit clause is possible if the gains are not reached at the other sites.

## Actions
- Recommendation to the COO by 30 June; contract decision by **15 July 2026**.
- HR to start the works council consultations now, whichever vendor is chosen.
